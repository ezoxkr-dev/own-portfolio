Undergraduate Research Deep Dive

Interpretable CNN-Transformer Architecture

Varendra University Department of CSE • Biosignal Affective Computing

Research Objective: EEG emotion recognition typically struggles with cross-subject variability and signal artifacts. This research models affective states (valence and arousal) across heterogeneous datasets (SEED and DEAP) without extensive manual feature engineering.

Three-Stage Pipeline Architecture:
• Stage 1 (1D-CNN): Extracts local temporal patterns and spatial correlation across 62 electrode channels.
• Stage 2 (Transformer Self-Attention): Computes long-range multi-head attention to capture emotional state transitions over time.
• Stage 3 (Classifier & Topomap Visualizer): Produces class probabilities and maps attention weights onto the 10-20 electrode layout.
